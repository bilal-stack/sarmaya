The **"OS Master ERP Blueprint v1"** is a ~10-12 page Google Doc (shared by the user, doc id `1Tj5ws_7cbUBPbNz0x5iVtE33EIwR_59nCWy2OYnC0c8`). It is the **most detailed and plain-English** of the three layered Sarmaya specs — deeper than the [[project-mvp-spec]] (contracted near-term) and [[project-build-book]] (vision/non-negotiables). Same governance-first philosophy.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked me to read it end-to-end to absorb full context before we start building. It fleshes out the *behavior* of every module/workflow that the Build Book only names.

**How to apply:** Use this as the functional-depth reference when implementing any module — it gives concrete states, guards, and AI guardrails. Still defer to the MVP PDF for what is *contracted now*; this blueprint describes the broader target. Flag ambiguities rather than guessing (Decision Register).

**Doc structure (Parts):**
- **Finance OS** — COA, Vendor Master, PO, GRN, Invoice+OCR, Matching Engine (2-way/3-way), Approval workflows, Payments (maker-checker, bank export), Bank Reconciliation, Finance Dashboard.
- **Procurement OS** — PR → RFQ → Quote Comparison → PO (auto from selected vendor) → GRN; vendor compliance/activation rules; full PR→RFQ→Quote→PO→GRN linkage required.
- **Part 5 Supply Chain OS** — Item Master, Warehousing (zones/racks/bins, in-transit/QC locations), Stock (on-hand/reserved/in-transit, FIFO/Weighted-Avg), GRN & Put-Away (cannot receive > PO qty unless policy allows), Picking & Dispatch + POD, Consumption (approved → GL expense), Stock Transfers (Requested→Approved→Picked→In-Transit→Received→Closed), Cycle Counts & Adjustments (dual approval, auto GL to Finance), Logistics (Scheduled→Loaded→On Route→Delivered→Failed→Returned).
- **Part 6 HR / Manpower / Hiring OS** (13 modules) — Job Openings; Candidate Pipeline Kanban (Applied→Screened→Shortlisted→Interview→Final→Offered→Selected→Onboarded); Onboarding (cannot onboard without docs/contract); Employee Master Record (HR equivalent of Vendor Master); Manpower/Project Assignment; Roster/Shift Planning; Attendance (manual/biometric via Integration Hub); Timesheets (Draft→Submitted→Validated→Approved→Locked); Leave Mgmt; **Payroll-lite (calculation only — Finance OS does the payment; locks after approval)**; HR compliance/expiry reminders (30d/7d/on-expiry).
- **Part 7 AI Agents & Automation Layer** — components: AI Router (model selector) + Policy Gate + Evidence Logger. 4 agent classes: A=Data Extraction (OCR, classifier, resume/bank-statement/GRN parsers), B=Workflow (validation, matching, approval suggestion, routing, exception detection, policy explainer), C=Decision Support (cash forecast, demand planning, reorder, vendor risk, duplicate detector, payroll prediction, attendance anomaly, candidate ranking), D=Conversational Copilots per domain. **Core rule: "Agents Assist. Policies Decide."** AI may suggest/flag/predict/explain/extract/match/summarize but ✗ cannot approve, move workflow states, create payments, change bank details, or override policy. Every AI action logged (name, trigger, in/out summary, confidence, model, latency, timestamp, who accepted/ignored, policy impact).
- **Part 8 Cross-Module Workflows** — master P2P chain Need→PR→Approval→RFQ→Vendor Select→PO→GRN→Invoice→3-Way Match→Approval→Payment→Bank→Reconciliation→Audit; parallel HR chain Hiring→Pipeline→Onboard→EMR→Roster/Timesheets→Payroll-lite→Finance Payment. Bidirectional linking required. Cross-module SoD (vendor creator ≠ invoice approver; PR creator ≠ PO approver; GRN handler ≠ invoice approver; payroll preparer ≠ approver).
- **Part 9 Acceptance Criteria** — 12 global reqs (tenant isolation, no workflow skipping, policy enforced before progress, evidence on every action, file hash/metadata, shared UX, cross-module links, dashboards <3s, AI cannot override policy, Integration Hub formats CSV/PDF/OCR/MT940/CAMT.053/Excel, mobile responsive). **AI accuracy targets:** OCR 80%, doc classifier 90%, GL suggestion 70%, duplicate detection 95%, matching 85%; AI outputs <2s avg. **Perf:** dashboards <3s, lists <1s, upload <4s, OCR <2s, workflow change <1s, API <400ms.
- **Part 10 Summary** — functional summaries of all 4 variants + **10 system-wide shared components**: Workflow Engine (state machine), Policy Engine (DB rules), Cross-Module Linking Layer, Evidence Layer, AI Explainability Layer, Multi-Tenant Architecture, Exceptions Management System, Model Router, Pack-Based Modular Architecture (Finance/Procurement/Supply-Chain/HR/Vendor/Reporting packs), HITL Safety Framework.
- **"AI in OS" appendix** — pragmatic, cost-conscious AI plan: do NOT train a giant finance LLM; orchestrate small bounded models under governance. Suggested open-source/API tools: OCR=LayoutLMv3/Donut/MS Form Recognizer; bank parsing=GROBID-Finance/DocTR/PDFPlumber; GL coding=FinBERT/CodeBERT/sklearn; forecasting=Prophet/XGBoost/NeuralProphet; anomaly=PyOD/Isolation Forest; chat=Llama-3/Mistral/Mixtral/Claude Haiku; explainability=SHAP/LIME. Orchestrate via LangChain/Haystack/LlamaIndex; serve via Ollama/vLLM + FastAPI microservices (/extract_invoice, /suggest_gl, /forecast_cash, /ask_finance); Postgres for prompts/results, MinIO/S3 for evidence. Roadmap v1 pre-trained plug-and-play → v2 RAG chatbot → v3 fine-tune on anonymized tenant data.
- **"Inspos"** — competitor/reference products: Medius, Sage, Esker, Tipalti, Acumatica, FinanceGPT, Planful, Trullion, SutiSoft, Stampli, Flow (getflow), Pageflows (wireframes). Notes: 3-way/2-way match is baseline; AI invoice capture + predictive coding expected; vendor onboarding/KYC a selling point (localize VAT/TRN, not US W-forms); bulk payment files CSV/ISO20022 pain.001 + statements MT940/camt.053 standard.
